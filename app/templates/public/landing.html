Features

Much more than event registration

Each feature solves a real problem in running events on campus.

QR attendance you can trust

Every registration gets a signed, unique QR code. The logistics team scans it and the server checks the registration, the event and any earlier check-in, so duplicates and forged codes are rejected.

An AI assistant that never guesses

Ask about timings, venue or rules. Answers come only from the official event details and the PDF the organizer uploaded. If it is not written there, the assistant says so.

Faculty oversight built in

Events are proposed, reviewed and approved by faculty before students ever see them, with remarks, readiness tracking and participation reports.

Logistics with a readiness score

A shared checklist covers venue, equipment, seating, refreshments and volunteers, and turns progress into a clear event readiness percentage.

How it works

One workflow, four roles

Each step hands over cleanly to the next team.

Organizer proposes

Create an event, upload the official PDF and submit it for approval.

Faculty reviews

Approve with confidence or reject with remarks. Approval opens the logistics checklist.

Event is published

Students discover it, register and receive their QR code.

Logistics runs the day

Tasks are completed, readiness reaches 100% and QR codes are scanned at entry.

Certificates are issued

Attendees receive verifiable certificates and faculty see the results.

Roles

Built for everyone involved

CollegeHub has exactly four roles, each with a focused workspace.

Students

  • Find and register for events
  • Show a personal QR code
  • Ask the AI assistant
  • Collect certificates and achievements

Organizers

  • Propose and manage events
  • Upload official documents
  • Approve volunteers
  • See registrations and attendance

Logistics

  • Work a shared checklist
  • Track readiness percentage
  • Scan QR codes at entry
  • Catch duplicate or invalid codes

Faculty

  • Review and approve proposals
  • Monitor participation
  • Publish announcements
  • Review event reports
