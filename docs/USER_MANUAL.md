# CollegeHub user manual

For students, organizers, the logistics team and faculty. No technical knowledge needed.

**Signing in**: open the CollegeHub address, choose **Log in**, enter your username (or email) and password. Students create their own account with **Create account** (username, email, password, confirm). The Organizer and Logistics accounts are not self-registered: the Faculty Mentor creates the Organizer account, and the Organizer creates the Logistics account. Use **Sign out** (bottom of the left menu) when you finish on a shared computer.

---

## Creating your student account

1. Choose **Create account**.
2. **Username** must look like `firstname.lastname`: lowercase letters, one dot, optionally a number at the end if the name is taken (for example `rahul.sharma` or `rahul.sharma01`). The form shows this format. If your username is taken you are told and a free one is suggested.
3. Enter your email, a password (at least 8 characters with a letter and a number) and confirm it.
4. You can sign in at once. Your account starts as **Verification pending**.

## Getting verified

Complete your profile (photo, phone, department, branch, semester, section, optional student ID, skills, interests, bio, links) and the Organizer will review it. Until you are verified you can browse events, read announcements and edit your profile, but **registering for official events, volunteering and certificates need verification**. If you try, the page explains why.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Waiting for the Organizer. |
| Verified | You are a **Verified CollegeHub Member** and get a blue badge. |
| Rejected | The Organizer gave a reason. Fix your profile and press **Resubmit**. |
| Suspended | Access is limited; contact the Organizer. |

On **Profile** you will find **Edit Profile**, **Change Password** and **Verification Status**. You cannot edit your role, verification or account status.

## For students

### Find an event
1. Choose **Explore events** (menu) or **Events** (top bar).
2. Type in the search box, or filter by category, club, and *Upcoming / Past / All*.
3. Select an event to see the date, time, venue, deadline, seats left and description.

### Register and get your QR code
1. Open the event and press **Register for this event**.
2. You land on your **registration page** showing your **Registration ID** (for example `CH-2026-004-7K2QX`) and a **QR code**.
3. Find it again any time under **My registrations → View**.

You cannot register twice, after the deadline, when the event is full, or for an event that is not published. The page tells you which reason applies.

### On the day
Show your QR code on your phone (or printed) to the logistics team at the entrance. They scan it and you are checked in. If your screen is damaged, read out your Registration ID. Do not share your QR code with anyone.

### Ask the AI Event Assistant
1. Open **AI Event Assistant** (or the "Ask the Event Assistant" box on an event page).
2. Choose the event and type a question, such as "When does the keynote start?"
3. The answer comes with its **source** (for example *AI-Summit-Official-Information.pdf, page 1*).
4. If the answer is not in the official information, the assistant says it **could not find it** and tells you who to ask. It never makes things up.

### Certificates, announcements, achievements, volunteering
- **Certificates**: appear after you attended an event and the organizer issued them. **PDF** downloads it; **Verify** opens the public check page.
- **Announcements**: messages from faculty and from organizers of events you joined.
- **Profile & achievements**: complete your profile to unlock *Profile Ready*. Other achievements unlock as you take part.
- **Volunteering**: open roles are listed with slots left. Press **Apply**, optionally add a message. The organizer approves or rejects; you see the status under *My applications*.
- **Participation history**: a timeline of everything you did.

## For organizers

There is exactly one Organizer account, created and managed by the Faculty Mentor. Use **Change password** in the account menu; you may be asked to change a temporary password at first sign-in.

### Verify students
**Member verification** has tabs for Pending, Verified, Rejected and Suspended. Search by name, username or email and filter by department. Open a student to read their profile and history, then **Approve**, **Reject** (reason required, optionally ask for a correction), or **Suspend** (reason required). Every action asks for confirmation and is recorded with your name, the reason and the time.

### Manage the Logistics account
**Logistics account** shows its status and lets you change the username and email, set a new password, generate a temporary password, and activate or deactivate it. The Logistics team cannot verify students or touch any credentials.

### Complete an event and issue certificates
After a published event has started, open it and choose **Mark completed**. Certificates can be issued only after that, and only to verified students whose attendance was recorded. You can also assign approved volunteers to roles.

### Create an event
1. **Create event** → fill in title, description, category, club (optional), venue, date, start and end time, capacity, registration deadline, optional cover image.
2. Press **Save draft** to keep working later, or **Save and submit for approval**.
3. The deadline must be before the event starts. The form explains any problem next to the field.

### Follow the approval
Open **My events → Manage**. The **Overview** tab shows the status:

| Status | What you can do |
|--------|-----------------|
| Draft | Edit, submit, or delete |
| Pending approval | Wait, or withdraw it back to a draft |
| Approved | Press **Publish event** when ready (students can then see it) |
| Published | Run the event |
| Rejected | Read the faculty remarks, edit, and resubmit |

### Upload the official document (powers the AI)
**Documents & AI** tab → choose a PDF → **Upload**. Use a PDF with real text (not a photo of a page). The AI assistant answers student questions from it. The page tells you if no readable text was found.

### Registrations, volunteers, logistics, certificates, announcements
- **Registrations**: list of students, status, **Export CSV**, cancel a registration if needed.
- **Volunteers**: create roles with slots; **Approve** or **Reject** applications.
- **Logistics**: view and update the checklist (available once approved).
- **Certificates**: after the event, press **Issue certificates to attendees**. Only students who were checked in by QR receive one.
- **Announcements**: post a message for the event's students.
- **Analytics** (menu): registrations, attendance and seat-fill per event.

## For the Logistics Team

### Prepare an event
1. **Dashboard** lists approved and published events with a **readiness bar**.
2. Open an event → **Checklist**. For each task set the person or team, **Status** (*Pending, In progress, Completed*), add notes, and press **Save**.
3. **Add a task** for anything extra. Readiness = completed tasks ÷ all tasks.

### Scan QR codes at the entrance
1. Open the event and press **Open QR scanner** (only published events).
2. Press **Start scanning** and allow camera access. Hold the student's QR code in view.
3. The result box shows:
   - **Green, Checked in**: welcome the student.
   - **Amber, Already checked in**: someone already used this code. Do not admit twice.
   - **Amber, Wrong event**: the student registered for a different event.
   - **Red, Invalid QR code**: not a CollegeHub code. Ask for the Registration ID and use **Manual entry**.
4. **Manual entry** accepts the Registration ID if the camera or QR is not usable.

> The camera needs a secure page (HTTPS) or `localhost`. If the camera will not open, use manual entry.

## For faculty

**Organizer account** (menu) is where the Faculty Mentor views the Organizer's status, changes the username and email, sets a new password, generates a temporary password, and activates or deactivates the account. Faculty do not need to verify students. Faculty may also mark events completed.

1. **Dashboard**: pending proposals, events awaiting publication, published events, overall attendance, logistics readiness.
2. **Event proposals** → **Review**: read the details, supporting documents and the organizer's earlier events.
3. Decide on the right-hand panel:
   - **Approve event**: creates the logistics checklist and notifies the organizer.
   - **Reject with remarks**: remarks are required, so the organizer knows what to fix.
4. **Monitor events**: every event with registered, attended and readiness. Open **Report** for participants and the checklist; **Print report** makes a paper copy.
5. **Announcements**: publish to everyone, students, organizers or the logistics team, optionally tied to an event.
6. **Clubs & activity**: published events and attendance for each club.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| "Access denied" | Your role does not include that page. Use the menu. |
| I cannot register | Read the message: deadline passed, event full, or not published. |
| QR camera will not start | Allow camera permission; use HTTPS or `localhost`; or use manual entry. |
| Assistant says it could not find the answer | The organizer has not published that detail. Ask them directly. |
| Forgot password (student) | Choose **Forgot password** on the login page and enter your email. If an account exists you receive a link that works once and expires (default 1 hour). |
| Forgot password (Organizer, Logistics, Faculty) | Faculty reset the Organizer's password, the Organizer resets the Logistics password. Faculty accounts are reset by the institution's IT contact (command line). |
| "Verification required" | You are not verified yet. Complete your profile and wait for the Organizer, or resubmit if rejected. |
